Usage: log-viewer [--filter QUERY] [--dump] [--levels LIST] [FILE|DIRECTORY|-]...
Pipe logs, follow multiple files, or recursively open directories. / filters, Enter details, ? help, q quit.
--dump prints matching lines without the TUI (until EOF).
Directories read regular files in sorted traversal order and discover new files.
-- ends options; use - to include stdin alongside files.
--levels selects error,warn,info,debug,unknown (comma-separated), or all/none.
Keys 1-5 toggle those groups; 0 restores all levels.
